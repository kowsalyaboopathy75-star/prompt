# 🚀 OfferGuard AI – Smart Job & Internship Scam Detector

### AI-Powered Recruitment Scam Detection Platform

OfferGuard AI is an AI-powered application designed to help students, freshers, and job seekers identify potentially fraudulent job offers, fake internships, and suspicious recruitment messages.

The platform analyses submitted job descriptions, internship offers, and recruitment messages to identify suspicious patterns and provide a risk assessment. Its goal is to promote safer job searching and improve awareness of online recruitment scams.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers encounter fake job opportunities, fraudulent internship offers, misleading recruitment messages, and suspicious hiring processes.

These scams can result in financial losses, misuse of personal information, and missed career opportunities.

OfferGuard AI aims to address this problem by providing an accessible tool for analysing recruitment-related content and highlighting potential warning signs.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

OfferGuard AI provides an intelligent platform where users can submit job descriptions, internship details, or recruitment messages for analysis.

The application examines the submitted content and helps users understand whether it contains suspicious indicators.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Job Offer Analysis:** Analyse job descriptions and recruitment offers.
- **Internship Scam Detection:** Identify potentially suspicious internship opportunities.
- **Recruitment Message Analysis:** Examine recruitment messages for warning signs.
- **Suspicious Pattern Detection:** Highlight potentially fraudulent recruitment patterns.
- **AI-Based Risk Assessment:** Provide an analysis of possible scam indicators.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple and accessible platform for students and job seekers.
