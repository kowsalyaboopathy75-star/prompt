Recruitment Security Guidelines

Prevention
  • Never pay upfront: Reputable employers never charge registration, laptop, training, or verification fees.
  • Verify domains independently: Confirm recruiter email domains match official corporate websites.
  • Beware instant selection: High-paying offers without any technical or structured interview are a strong red flag.
  • Protect identity documents: Never share Aadhaar, PAN, SSN, passport scans, bank PINs, or OTPs over WhatsApp/Telegram.
  • Reject artificial urgency: Countdown threats ("pay within 2 hours") aim to bypass verification.
